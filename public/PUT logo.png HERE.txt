Put the studio logo here, named exactly:  logo.png

It appears in the header and footer of every page.
A PNG with a transparent background works best, roughly 600 x 200 pixels.
Tip: a light-coloured logo is hard to see on the light header, and a dark logo
is hard to see on the dark footer, so a logo with some colour works best.

Until it is added, the built-in "i Fotoz" mark is shown.
Once the admin dashboard is connected, the logo can also be uploaded in
Admin -> Website Settings -> Branding.
